JERTS CART is a student-only marketplace where you can buy and sell items within your university community. Think of it as your campus classifieds — but simpler and safer.

Anyone with a verified university email (.edu or your university's domain) can create an account and list items. We verify every account to keep the community safe.

Payments happen directly between buyers and sellers. Most students use cash, mobile money (MoMo), or bank transfer when they meet on campus. JERTS CART doesn't handle payments — we just connect you.
